# Migros Switzerland Products
A selection of products and their information from Migros Switzerland.

Due to the structure of the Migros product catalogue only a part of it is scraped.
- **Only all products of Migros region Zurich are scraped.**
- No other regions or their online catalogue is included.
- If there is a wish for every region to be scraped or something else please leave a comment. 

Product information contains:
- Name
- Price
- Price Text
- Unit of product
- Unit price
- If it is discounted
- Discount information
- Product category
- Image URL
- Product URL
