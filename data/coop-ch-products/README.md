# Coop Switzerland Products
All products and their information from Coop Switzerland.

The entire online catalogue is included.

Product information contains:
- Name
- Price
- Price Text
- Unit of product
- Unit price
- If it is discounted
- Discount information
- Product category
- Image URL
- Product URL
